I joined the Working Draft podcast to talk about HTTP/3 and WebTransport.

We traced the evolution from HTTP/1 through HTTP/2 to HTTP/3, why head-of-line blocking pushed the web toward QUIC, and how building transport and security together enables 1-RTT and 0-RTT handshakes, connection migration, and encrypted DNS. We closed on WebTransport as a modern alternative to WebSocket, supporting both streams and datagrams.

The episode is in German.
